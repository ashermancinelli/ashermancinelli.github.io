# History of Compilers
_10/23/2025_

I feel that the history of compiler has not yet been told _as such_, but only in fragments,
as a subplot of other stories.
I would love to read such a book if it existed.
This PDF is a collection of my personal research on the book that I wish existed--one
on the history of compilers _in particular_.
