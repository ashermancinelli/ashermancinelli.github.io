# Style Inspiration

There are many authors I appreciate, but I don't intend to imitate all of them.
Jorge Luis Borges has a unique style that is both poetic and precise, but it can be dense and confusing;
it is perfectly well-suited to short stories.

The following authors meet at least two criteria: I find them well-spoken, _and_ I would like to imitate their styles and voice.

# Matt Levine

- His voice and humor are not so over-the-top that they distract from the content or damage his credibility.

# Joe Wiesenthal

- Very similar to Matt Levine, but with far less written content available.
- He used to run a blog _The Stalwart_, but now most of his written content is paywalled at Bloomberg.

# Ken Thompson

- His precision and care for formatting and typesetting are unparalleled.
