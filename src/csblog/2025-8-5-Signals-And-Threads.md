# Signals and Threads w Chris Lattner

*9/15/2025*

There's a meme format on TikTok where a boyfriend will pretend to talk to someone on the phone while their girlfriend is minding her business in the same room (or vice-versa).

The boyfriend will start making plans with someone on the phone, mentioning all his girlfriend's interests and hobbies, and the girlfriend will get increasingly intersted in the conversation, trying to figure out what she's being left out of.
That's a bit how I felt listening to this episode of Signals and Threads.

So, this where I'm dumping all the thoughts I had while listening to the episode.


## An Engineer's Perspective

I appreciate that Chris is as acomplished as he is in compilers and PLT without dipping into the langauge that PLT nerds often use (I say this with affection for PLT nerds).

Chris explained how he takes on a pragmatic bottom-up approach to type theory and language design rather than taking type theory as a starting point.
Chris comes off to me as an engineer that broke into PLT.
I relate to Chris.

## Curiosity-Driven Development
